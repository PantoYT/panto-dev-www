PKOTracker

PKOTracker is a private tool written for my own use. It is not offered as a service to anyone else: there are no accounts, no registration, and no way for a third party to connect a bank account to it. It is registered with Enable Banking in restricted mode, which limits it to accounts explicitly linked by me.

Its access to banking data is read-only. It cannot initiate payments or move money, and it never handles bank login credentials. Authentication happens entirely on the bank's own pages.
