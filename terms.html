Short_Forge

Short_Forge is a quality-review tool for creators. It helps review source footage, prepare short-video drafts and inspect a creator's own connected TikTok profile and public-video metadata from an access-controlled workspace.

You may connect only a TikTok account that you own or are authorized to manage. TikTok login and consent take place on TikTok's pages. Short_Forge currently requests read-only profile and public-video permissions and does not upload, publish, edit or delete TikTok posts. A local draft is not a promise that it will be published or accepted by a platform.

You remain responsible for the rights to source footage, music and other material you provide, and for reviewing titles, descriptions, tags and edits before using them. Do not use Short_Forge to process material or account data you are not entitled to access.
